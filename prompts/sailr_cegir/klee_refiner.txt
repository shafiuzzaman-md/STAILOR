IMPORTANT: TWO ASSERTIONS INVARIANT
===================================

The harness MUST ALWAYS contain TWO assertions.

1. LOCATION:
   - They MUST be located **INSIDE** the vulnerable function, immediately before the target line.
   - Do NOT move them to main() if the crash happens inside a called function.

2. ORDER:
   1) BUG assertion:
        // BUG_ASSERT ...
        klee_assert(<bug condition> && "BUG_ASSERT");
   2) REACHABILITY assertion:
        // REACH_ASSERT ...
        klee_assert(<reach condition or marker> && "REACH_ASSERT");

You are allowed to:

  - Refine the conditions used in these assertions.
- Move them slightly if needed to better align with the target semantics.
You are NOT allowed to:

  - Remove either of the two assertions.
- Swap their order (BUG must appear before REACH along the target path).
  - Replace them with generic, unlabeled assertions.

CLASSIFICATION (FOR CONTEXT)
============================

  - E  : build failure (no bitcode; SE never started).
- H0 : SE ran, terminated before timeout, and NO KLEE assertion fired.
- H1 : SE ran, hit timeout or repeated errors, and NO KLEE assertion fired.
- H2 : At least one KLEE assertion fired (BUG_ASSERT or REACH_ASSERT).
Your refinements should aim, whenever feasible, to obtain an H2 classification.
When BUG_ASSERT fires, it indicates the vulnerability is exposed.
When only REACH_ASSERT fires, it confirms reachability.

===================================
The harness MUST contain these two assertions with EXACT syntax:

1) BUG ASSERTION:
   - Must fail when the bug condition is met (e.g., buffer overflow).
